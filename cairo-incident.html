INCIDENT SUMMARY

INITIAL RECORD

The Cairo Incident marks the first confirmed appearance of an individual later identified within Evrane records as Mirage.

Details surrounding the circumstances of the event remain incomplete. Surviving records indicate anomalous activity beneath Cairo, followed by a series of events later connected to Malik Sayeed.

Much of the original documentation remains sealed while the full nature of the incident continues to be assessed.
