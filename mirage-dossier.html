ARCHIVE ASSESSMENT

OBSERVED CAPABILITIES

Mirage possesses the ability to manifest autonomous duplicates referred to within Evrane records as Echoes.

Each Echo appears capable of independent movement while remaining linked to Malik through a shared sensory connection. Recorded observations indicate that Malik can perceive the environment through their senses and coordinate multiple Echoes simultaneously.

The full limits of the phenomenon remain undocumented.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

FEEDBACK EFFECT

Damage sustained by an Echo produces a corresponding sensory response in Malik. Although no equivalent physical injury has been recorded, repeated impacts can cause pain, disorientation and loss of concentration.

Increased numbers of active Echoes appear to intensify the cognitive strain associated with maintaining the connection.
