Malik Sayeed never intended to become a Paragon.

A student living in Cairo, Malik's life is changed by events that leave him with abilities he does not fully understand. What begins as something impossible to explain soon forces Malik into a world far larger than his own.

ORIGIN

THE BIRTH OF ECHO

Malik Sayeed's abilities first manifested beneath the streets of Cairo. What happened there remains difficult to explain — even to Malik. He remembers the place, the moment, and the impossible sensation that something around him had changed. What he did not understand was that something within him had changed with it.

At first, the changes were almost imperceptible movements at the edge of his vision, reflections that seemed to move without him, fleeting glimpses of himself where no one stood. Then one of them remained. It moved when Malik did not. For the first time, he realised he was not looking at a reflection of himself, but something he had brought into the world. In time, he would come to call them Echoes.

As Malik learned to control the Echoes, he discovered that the connection ran both ways. He could see through their eyes, hear what they heard and feel the world through their senses. But that connection carried a cost. Every impact they suffered travelled back to him, not as physical injury, but as pain, shock and disorientation. The more Echoes he created, the more minds, movements and sensations he was forced to carry at once.

CHARACTER

BEHIND THE MASK

Malik Sayeed is observant, thoughtful and quietly ambitious. He tends to notice the details others overlook, a trait shaped by his fascination with architecture and the influence of his father, Omar. He is rarely the loudest person in the room, but he is often one of the most aware.

Around the people he trusts, Malik is warmer and more playful than his reserved nature initially suggests. His younger sister Nour brings this side of him out more than anyone. Their relationship is one of the most important parts of his life, and Malik carries a strong instinct to protect her even when she does not need protecting.

Becoming Mirage complicates that instinct. Malik dislikes feeling responsible for other people, yet finds it increasingly difficult to ignore situations where he knows he could make a difference. He does not see himself as fearless. He questions his decisions, makes mistakes and sometimes hesitates when the consequences become real.

What ultimately defines Malik is not his powers, but his ability to adapt. He watches, learns and changes his approach rather than simply overpowering whatever stands in front of him. As Mirage, that same instinct becomes his greatest strength: making others see what he wants them to see while he searches for the solution they never noticed.

FAMILY

THE SAYEEDS

For Malik, family has always been the centre of his world. Long before Paragons, Echoes or Mirage, the Sayeed family shaped the person he would become.

FATHER · ARCHITECT

OMAR SAYEED

Omar Sayeed is an architect whose influence can be seen in Malik's fascination with design and the structures of Cairo. From him, Malik developed an eye for detail and a desire to understand how the world around him is constructed — an interest that has led him towards ambitions of following his father into architecture.

MOTHER · ARCHAEOLOGIST / UNIVERSITY LECTURER

DR. LAYLA SAYEED

Layla's fascination with Egyptian history has taken her into the world of archaeological discoveries. Intelligent, knowledgeable and deeply invested in the past, she encouraged Malik's curiosity from an early age, even if the two were drawn towards understanding the world in very different ways.

YOUNGER SISTER

NOUR SAYEED

Nour brings out a side of Malik few others see. Around her, his usually reserved nature gives way to something warmer and more playful. The two share a close relationship, although Malik's tendency to protect her can occasionally cause friction between them.

Becoming Mirage changes those relationships. As Malik keeps his new life hidden, protecting his family increasingly means lying to them.
