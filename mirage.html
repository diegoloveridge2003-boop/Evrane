THE BOY BEHIND THE MIRAGE

Malik Sayeed never intended to become a Paragon.

A student living in Cairo, Malik's life is changed by events that leave him with abilities he does not fully understand. What begins as something impossible to explain soon forces Malik into a world far larger than his own.

SECONDARY WEAKNESS

FEEDBACK

When an Echo takes damage, Malik experiences the sensation of the impact. Although his physical body is not injured, the pain and sensory shock are real. Repeated impacts can overwhelm him and disrupt his concentration.

ORIGIN

THE BIRTH OF ECHO

Malik Sayeed's abilities first manifested beneath the streets of Cairo. What happened there remains difficult to explain — even to Malik. He remembers the place, the moment, and the impossible sensation that something around him had changed. What he did not understand was that something within him had changed with it.

At first, the changes were almost imperceptible — movements at the edge of his vision, reflections that seemed to move without him, fleeting glimpses of himself where no one stood. Then one of them remained. It moved when Malik did not. For the first time, he realised he was not looking at a reflection of himself, but something he had brought into the world. In time, he would come to call them Echoes.

As Malik learned to control the Echoes, he discovered that the connection ran both ways. He could see through their eyes, hear what they heard and feel the world through their senses. But that connection carried a cost. Every impact they suffered travelled back to him — not as physical injury, but as pain, shock and disorientation. The more Echoes he created, the more minds, movements and sensations he was forced to carry at once.
